Data and model

Predicting the net electrical output of a combined-cycle power plant from ambient conditions.

Dataset

Combined Cycle Power Plant, P. Tüfekci and H. Kaya, UCI Machine Learning Repository, 2014, doi:10.24432/C5002N, licence CC BY 4.0. 9,568 hourly rows recorded at full load.

Checks

  • Missing values: 0.
  • Exact duplicate rows: 41 (kept; 0.4 % of rows, plausible repeated readings).

Split

Training set: first 7,654 rows. Test set: last 1,914 rows, used once for the final error.
