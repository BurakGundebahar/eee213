§ 1.1 Model

Schematic

Fig. 1 · DC source, two linear resistors, two cubic non-linear resistors to ground
  • Steady DC state; ideal source and wires.
  • Linear resistors obey Ohm's law.
  • Non-linear elements are exactly cubic, I = K·V³, independent of temperature (varistor-like).
  • Node voltages are positive.

§ 1.3 Equations

KCL + Jacobian

Kirchhoff's current law at each node; every term in A.

f1 = (V1 − Vs)/R1 + K1·V1³ + (V1 − V2)/R2 = 0
f2 = (V2 − V1)/R2 + K2·V2³              = 0

Jacobian, derived by hand; entries in A/V.

      ┌ 1/R1 + 1/R2 + 3·K1·V1²     −1/R2             ┐
J  =  │                                              │
      └ −1/R2                      1/R2 + 3·K2·V2²   ┘

Newton step:   J·ΔV = −f     V ← V + ΔV
Start:         V1 = V2 = 1 V
Stop:          |ΔV| < 1e-9 V
§ 1.4 Verification

Independent check

  • From f2: V1 = V2 + R2·K2·V2³. Substituting into f1 leaves one equation in V2, solved by bisection on 0–10 V — a different method that must give the same V1, V2.
  • KCL residuals |f1|, |f2| must be close to zero.
  • Power balance: source power equals total power dissipated in R1, R2 and both non-linear elements.
